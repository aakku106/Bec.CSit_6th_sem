# E-Government Model

## Section A

**Attempt any TWO question.**

1. What is E-Government Life Cycle? Explain each stage in detail. How can E-Governance help bridge the digital divide and ensure inclusive service delivery in Nepal’s diverse geographical and socio-economic context? Explain with suitable examples.

2. What is E-Government Architecture and Interoperability Framework? Why are these crucial for successful e-governance implementation? Explain different types of network infrastructure and computing infrastructure required with examples from Nepal’s e-governance projects.

3. Explain Mobilization and Lobbying Model and Government-to-Citizen-to-Government (G2C2G) Interactive Service Model in detail. How can these models be used to enhance citizen participation in governance, especially in the context of recent youth-led movements in Nepal?

## Section B

4. What is the difference between online service delivery and electronic service delivery? How does e-governance promote SMART government? Justify with examples.
