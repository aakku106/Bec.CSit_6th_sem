## Study Priority Summary

1. **Master first:** Electronic Payment Systems ([[E-Com/Syllabus#Unit 3. Electronic Payment System (9 Hrs.)\|Unit 3]] — digital wallet, credit cards, e-checks, SET), Security threats & mechanisms ([[E-Com/Syllabus#Unit 5. Security in E-Commerce (7 Hrs.)\|Unit 5]]), Business Models & EDI ([[E-Com/Syllabus#Unit 2. E-commerce Business Model (8 Hrs.)\|Unit 2]]), Building E-commerce systems/catalogs ([[E-Com/Syllabus#Unit 4. Building E-commerce System (5 Hrs.)\|Unit 4]]), and core Introduction concepts — types, features, ETA of Nepal ([[E-Com/Syllabus#Unit 1. Introduction (4 Hrs.)\|Unit 1]]). All five appear in literally every paper.
2. **Then cover:** Digital Marketing ([[E-Com/Syllabus#Unit 6. Digital Marketing (7 Hrs.)\|Unit 6]]) and SEO/Recommendation Systems ([[E-Com/Syllabus#Unit 7. Optimizing E-commerce Systems (5 Hrs.)\|Unit 7]]) — both appear in 4 of 5 papers.
3. **Last, if time remains:** SET protocol fine details (dual signature, payment authorization/capture), auctioning types, virtual currency, exchanges vs industry consortiums, omni-channel, competitive advantage dimensions — and skip the legacy 2076-only topics unless you have extra time to spare.

> Note: Every paper pulls roughly one question from each unit, so the syllabus is tested very evenly — Tier 1 topics simply repeat with slightly different framing each year (e.g. "digital wallet" becomes "SET payment authorization" becomes "dual signature"), so understanding Unit 3 (Electronic Payment Systems) and Unit 5 (Security) deeply will cover a large share of marks regardless of exact phrasing.
