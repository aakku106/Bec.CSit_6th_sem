**Section A**

**Attempt any three questions.**

1

Suppose you are working as an IT expert in the National Bank of Nepal, and working staff need five laptops for daily operation. Your manager asks you to present a proposal with time, cost, and objectives. Write a short proposal comparing the laptops available in the market of at least three renowned companies. Your proposal must include problems, solutions, and findings.

2

As writing is not an overnight endeavor it follows a certain process. Discuss a process for technical writing.

3

You have watched one of your peer’s vlog (video blog) entries on the issue of “Blocked mind as a malfunction of communication”. What have you learned in addition to your ideas you learned from your teacher? How do your experiences in unblocking the mind differ from your peer? Write feedback to your peer’s vlog.

4

What are graphics? How are they used in technical writing?
